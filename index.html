FAQ

Questions You
Might Be Thinking

Everything you need to know before we start building your digital growth engine.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites are completed within 7–21 days depending on the complexity and scope. Simple static sites can go live in as little as 5–7 days, while custom dynamic platforms typically take 2–4 weeks. We'll provide a precise timeline after our initial consultation.

Do you work with businesses outside Chandigarh?

Absolutely. While we're based in Chandigarh, we work with businesses across India and internationally. Our entire process — from strategy to delivery — is built to work seamlessly remotely.

What makes KAASNT different from other agencies?

We combine design excellence with performance marketing and SEO under one roof. We're not just vendors — we're growth partners who stay invested in your results. Every decision we make is tied to measurable business outcomes.

How does the SEO process work?

We start with a comprehensive SEO audit, followed by competitor analysis and keyword research. We then implement on-page, technical, and off-page optimizations in phases, with monthly reporting so you always see progress.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance and support?

Yes. All plans include a support period and we offer ongoing maintenance packages for updates, security, performance monitoring, and content changes. Your website stays fast, secure, and up-to-date.

Can I see pricing without contacting you?

Our pricing plans above give a great overview of what's included at each tier. Since every project has unique requirements, we don't publish fixed rates — but we're always transparent. Contact us for a detailed quote with no obligation.
